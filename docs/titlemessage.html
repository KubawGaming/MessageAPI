TitleMessage

Display large titles and subtitles in the center of the screen.

Configuration (YAML)

Full Example

welcome-title:
  title: "<gold><bold>WELCOME"
  subtitle: "<gray>to our server, {PLAYER}!"
  fadeIn: 10
  stay: 60
  fadeOut: 20
  commands:
  - "give {PLAYER} diamond 1"
  messageDelay: 40
  sound:
    paths:
    - "ui.toast.challenge_complete"
    - "entity.player.levelup"
    delay: 10
    volume: 1.0
    pitch: 1.2
Note: Both title and subtitle are optional. You can omit either field, and it will be treated as empty.

Minimal Example (Title Only)

simple-title:
  title: "<red><bold>WARNING!"
  fadeIn: 10
  stay: 40
  fadeOut: 10

Developer Usage

Basic Usage

TitleMessage message = new TitleMessage("<gold><bold>WELCOME", "<gray>Enjoy your stay!", 10, 60, 20);
message.sendTo(player);

With Placeholders

TitleMessage message = new TitleMessage(
    "<aqua>Player Stats",
    "<gray>Level: {LEVEL} | Kills: {KILLS}",
    10, 60, 20
);
message.replace("{LEVEL}", player.getLevel())
       .replace("{KILLS}", 150)
       .sendTo(player);

Title Only (No Subtitle)

TitleMessage message = new TitleMessage("<red><bold>WARNING!", null, 10, 40, 10);
message.sendTo(player);
